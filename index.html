Engineering for work at sea.

I am a naval architect and structural engineer with over nine years in offshore and marine engineering. My work covers loadout and seafastening, lifting and rigging, tow stability, deck strength, and putting launch and recovery systems (LARS) and survey equipment onto vessels.

I have led engineering teams, been the client's main contact on several projects and checked the work of junior engineers. On one project for Safeen (for ADNOC), I led the planning and coordination to transport and load out more than 3,000 concrete subsea sleepers.

I studied Naval Architecture and Offshore Engineering at AMET University, Chennai. I speak English, Malayalam and Hindi.

What I do

  • 01

    Marine Operations

    Loadout engineering, seafastening design, tow stability analysis, bollard pull checks and barge longitudinal strength checks.

  • 02

    Lifting & Rigging

    Lift plans and crane capacity studies, rigging with spreader bar and padeye design, and pile, conductor and riser upending.

  • 03

    Structural Engineering

    Structural design and analysis, deck strength checks and reinforcement, grillages, mezzanine decks and equipment foundations.

  • 04

    Naval Architecture

    Stability booklets, scantling calculations, tonnage and stability checks, and EEDI/EEXI compliance.

  • 05

    Vessel Mobilisation

    LARS and survey equipment: seafastening and grillage design, main deck strength checks, stability addenda and class approval.

  • 06

    Delivery & Approvals

    Calculations, reports and fabrication drawings, with coordination across class (ABS, DNV, LR) and marine warranty surveyors.
